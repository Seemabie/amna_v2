I. About these sessions

What I bring,
what you don't need to.

I arrive with two cameras, soft lights for cloudy days, a small kit of cream and oatmeal wraps, and tea if you'd like some. Most sessions need none of the props; the most beautiful frames are almost always your baby on your own duvet, in the light from your own window.

You don't need to clean. You don't need to dress up. You don't need to know what to do with your hands. The session moves at the baby's pace, and a feed or a nap is part of the rhythm, not an interruption.
