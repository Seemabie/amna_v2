III. The process

From inquiry
to archive.

Working together is meant to feel like a conversation, not a transaction. Here is the shape of it, from the first email to the heirloom prints.

i.

Reserve

Choose your session and a date. A simple form, no back and forth required.

ii.

Prepare

A wardrobe and location guide, then a short call to plan the rhythm of the day.

iii.

Photograph

Unhurried, often quiet, always patient. Children lead. Parents are allowed to breathe.

iv.

Receive

Edited gallery in three to four weeks. Prints and albums, if you'd like them, follow.
