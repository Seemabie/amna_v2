I. About these sessions

Six to ten hours,
and the album to follow.

Most weddings I photograph run between six and ten hours, from the getting-ready morning to the first hour of the reception. An engagement session is included in every wedding booking, used either as a wardrobe rehearsal or, more often, just as a chance for us to walk together before the day.

I shoot quietly. I am happy in the corner. I will not direct your aunt to look at the camera. The album that arrives in six to eight weeks will feel like the day itself, only edited toward its golden frames.
